# Equipment reference

## Console (Tempo T5-L)

- Inputs: start input, two deck harness connectors (start end), four turn-end harness connectors.
- Outputs: 1/4 inch legacy scoreboard jack, two RS-485 scoreboard outputs, USB Type-B results port, Ethernet port for the setup laptop.
- Power: 12 V DC supply, 7 A.
- The Legacy variant has the 1/4 inch jack; the Serial variant has RS-485 outputs only.

## Deck harness (H8-8)

- One breakout per lane at the pool edge, each with a pad socket and three backup button sockets (A, B, C).
- The start system cable joins the harness at the lane 1 breakout, which has an extra socket marked START.
- The harness suffix is the lane count: an H8-8 serves 8 lanes, an H8-10 serves 10.

## Touch pads

- Pads hang from the gutter on two hooks. Pads must hang straight; a tilted pad can miss light touches.
- Pad cables have a waterproof plug. Fit the plug cover whenever a pad is unplugged.
- Store pads flat. Rolling them damages the sensing layer.

## Backup buttons

- Each button has a coiled cable about 3 m long.
- Buttons are color coded: A red, B blue, C yellow.
- Replace a button whose press registers twice in diagnostics; that is a failing switch.

## Start system

- Horn and strobe in one unit, powered by a rechargeable battery.
- A full charge lasts about two sessions. Charge it overnight before each meet day.
- The start signal is sent on the cable when the trigger is pressed; the horn sounds at the same time.

## Scoreboard

- Outdoor LED matrix, 10 lines by 32 characters.
- BoardLink drives it through the scoreboard laptop; the board has no controls of its own.
- The board shows lane, place, name, club and time, with event and heat on the top line.

## Laptops

- Setup laptop: Windows, Tempo Swim v3.2.1, wired Ethernet through a USB adapter.
- Scoreboard laptop: Windows, BoardLink v2.4.0, USB serial adapter in the left port.
- Results laptop: Windows, MeetDesk 6.1, USB cable to the console's results port.
- All three laptops are set never to sleep during meets.

## Spares kept at the timing desk

- One spare touch pad, two spare backup buttons (one red, one yellow).
- A spare 1/4 inch scoreboard cable, 15 m.
- A spare USB serial adapter, labeled SB-2.
- The spare horn battery, in the grey case under the timing desk.
