# Meet day checklist

## Two hours before

- Power up the console, the setup laptop, the scoreboard laptop and the results laptop.
- Confirm Tempo Swim finds the console, and that the pool is set to 8 lanes, start end only, 25 m.
- Load the start lists from the USB key into the console and into BoardLink.
- Check every pad with a hand touch and every backup button from diagnostics.

## One hour before

- Run a one-lap test race. Confirm times appear on the board and can be pulled into MeetDesk.
- Fire the start system and confirm the clock starts.
- Set Sleep and Screen to Never on all laptops.

## During the meet

- Save and Reset after every heat.
- Keep the deck harness connectors dry and covered between sessions.
- If a laptop needs a reboot, re-check its COM port setting afterwards.

## After the meet

- Back up the meet database from MeetDesk to the USB key.
- Power down the laptops before the console.
